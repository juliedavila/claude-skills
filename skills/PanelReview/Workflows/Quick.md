# Quick Workflow

Parallel quick reads, merged to one screen, for a first take or limited time.

## Steps

1. Collect the package and settle the facts as in the Panel workflow, steps 1 and 2.
2. Choose the seats from `Routing.md`; for Quick, at most four.
3. Brief every seat from `Brief.md` with the workflow set to Quick and the output path still named (a Quick seat writes its chat-format result to the file as well, so the merge reads from disk).
4. Launch every seat at once in the background; serial fallback as in Panel step 5.
5. Read every seat's file in full.
6. Merge per the "Quick merge" section of `Merge.md`, and return in chat:
   - **Header:** artifact · reader and medium · seats · mode (parallel or serial).
   - **Overall read:** two or three sentences across the layers.
   - **Keep:** three folded strengths with seats and citations.
   - **Fix:** up to five folded holes, ranked, each with its seats, a citation, one line of concern and one line of suggestion.
   - **Conflicts:** one line each, with the winner and why.
   - **Order of work:** one line per layer.
   - **The question:** the single question across seats whose answer would most change the assessment.
   - **Offer:** the full Panel workflow, and any artifact a seat could not read.
