# Instrument — tell whether capacity is actually rising

**Gate:** a verdict on whether learning capacity has changed, resting on at least one signal that
exists outside the person's own sense of how it is going.

## The honest framing, stated first

The instruments here are coarse and slow, and a confident number would be dishonest. The source
material this skill draws on is full of precise-sounding figures for exactly this, essentially all
of them asserted without a source, including the reasonable-sounding ones. We are not going to
replace invented precision with our own.

What is available is a set of qualitative signals that are hard to fake and a few countable things
that drift cannot leave untouched. That is enough to answer "better, same, or worse" over a
quarter. It is not enough to answer "by how much," and this workflow will not pretend otherwise.

## Steps

### 1. Reject the activity metrics outright

Hours studied, pages covered, cards reviewed, sessions completed and streak length all survive
drift completely intact. A practice that has quietly stopped doing cognitive work scores exactly
the same on every one of them, which is what makes them worse than useless here: they provide
false reassurance precisely when the check matters most.

Count them for adherence if you like. Never count them as capacity.

### 2. Run the capacity signals

Each of these is hard to fake because each requires producing something cold.

**Time to first structure.** Given genuinely new material, how long before you can state its
organising logic rather than its contents? This is the clearest single signal of rising capacity
and it shortens noticeably over months when things are working.

**Unaided explanation depth.** Take something learned three months ago. Explain the mechanism
cold. Rising capacity shows as more mechanism and less vocabulary, not as more confidence.

**Confusion resolution.** Look back at confusions logged at the end of sessions over the period.
Were they resolved, did they turn out to be the wrong question, or are they still sitting there
untouched? A backlog of untouched confusions is the most honest bad news available.

**Transfer.** Has anything learned in one domain been used in another, unprompted? This is the
slowest signal and the most meaningful, because it is nearly impossible to produce by going
through motions.

**Better refusals.** Has triage got sharper? Saying no faster and with better reasons is real
capacity growth and is invisible to every conventional metric.

### 3. Check the decision record

Find one real decision made in the period that went differently because of something learned.
Name it specifically. If no decision changed across a whole quarter of study, that is the finding,
and it usually means the triage was routing to conviction things that only ever needed reference.

### 4. Give a three-way verdict, with its basis

Better, same, or worse, and say which signals carried the verdict. If the signals conflict, report
the conflict rather than averaging it; conflicting signals usually mean capacity rose in one area
while a different one drifted, and the average hides exactly the information worth having.

### 5. Change one thing

At most one. Multiple simultaneous changes mean the next instrument run cannot attribute anything,
and that turns a slow instrument into a useless one.

## Output

The verdict, the signals behind it, the decision that changed or the admission that none did, and
the single change being made.

## Gotchas

- **Quarterly at the fastest.** These signals move slowly, and measuring monthly generates noise
  that reads as signal and provokes unnecessary changes. Resist the pull toward a dashboard.
- **Do not build a scoring system.** The temptation to turn these into a number is strong and it
  reproduces the exact error this whole project documented in the source material. Qualitative
  and honest beats quantified and invented.
- **A "worse" verdict is usually a load problem rather than a method problem.** Check what else
  was happening in the quarter before changing the method. Methods rarely degrade; available
  attention does.
- **If every signal is unmeasurable, that is itself the result.** It means nothing was being
  produced cold, which means drift has probably been running for a while. Route to
  `Workflows/DriftCheck.md` rather than forcing a verdict.
