# Quick Workflow

A fast read of one artifact, for when the user wants a first take or has limited time. It reviews the one artifact it is given; sibling files it links to are not read, and the Offer at the end names the ones that would settle open holes.

## What Quick loads

`Intake.md` for reading the artifact and the map, `Lenses.md` for the lenses, and the **Rules for each finding** section of `ReviewFormat.md` (severity, no orphan critiques, no slogans, faithful quotes, the escape-point rule). Quick does not use ReviewFormat's file rule or its full template: the result goes in chat, no file unless asked, in the skeleton below.

## Steps

1. Read the artifact fully (`Intake.md` for type handling; still read deck notes). Decide which meaning of safety the artifact uses.
2. Write a short map: problem, change, decision asked, hazards named (those stated and those a reviewer would expect). The map is delivered, not working notes. If the decision asked is not stated, infer it in the map and move on; Intake's automatic Low hole for an unstated ask does not apply in Quick.
3. Skim `Lenses.md` and pick the three lenses that bite hardest. If the artifact adds or changes a defect source, lens 3 is one of them. If it puts an agent or automation in a position to act, lenses 1 and 7 apply together and share one of the three slots: fold the controller question into lens 7 and say so. Record the lenses skipped in one clause each.
4. Return, in chat, in this shape:
   - **Header:** artifact and version or date · stage, stated or inferred · decision asked · safety as used here · lenses applied and skipped.
   - **Map:** the short map from step 2.
   - **Overall read:** two or three sentences.
   - **Keep:** the top three strengths, each with a citation and the mechanism that makes it good.
   - **Fix:** up to five holes, ranked by consequence, with no severity labels. Each as one line of concern plus one line of suggestion, with a citation and the lens. Add an **Escape point** only on a hole about a defect that reached or would reach a later stage than it should, naming the stage that should have caught it.
   - **The question:** the single question whose answer would most change the assessment.
   - **Offer:** the linked or sibling artifacts that would confirm or retire the open holes, and the full Review workflow if the holes suggest the package needs it.
